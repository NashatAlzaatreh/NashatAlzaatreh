## 🎯 Profile

I lead **performance testing initiatives from strategy and planning through execution, analysis, and release recommendations**. My work brings together hands-on technical delivery, consultation, and test governance to help teams build responsive, stable, and scalable systems.

I partner with business and technical stakeholders to define meaningful non-functional requirements, establish performance acceptance standards, and turn test findings into decisions. Alongside this, I design realistic workloads, develop advanced scripts, coordinate distributed execution, and investigate performance bottlenecks.

As a team lead, I mentor engineers, strengthen delivery standards, and establish repeatable performance engineering practices across projects.
